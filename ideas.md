# Trio Bean Cafe — Design Direction

## Theme
**Roasted Editorial** — a warm, tactile cafe world where espresso craft meets quiet Indian morning light.

## Design movement
Editorial hospitality with art-directed photography, asymmetrical rhythm, and generous cream space. The page should feel like opening a well-made coffee table magazine: calm, tactile, premium, never fussy.

## Core principles
- Lead with atmosphere, then let the menu convert.
- Use contrast between quiet cream space and deep roasted ink.
- Make the phone CTA unmistakable and easy to reach.
- Keep motion soft, purposeful, and respectful of reduced-motion preferences.

## Color philosophy
- Roasted ink: #241A17
- Espresso brown: #6F4431
- Terracotta: #B65F42
- Saffron cream: #F2EBDD
- Milk white: #FBF8F2
- Moss accent: #6D7556

## Layout paradigm
A full-bleed hero with a floating navigation bar, followed by offset editorial sections. Use a 12-column feeling on desktop, collapsing to a single thoughtful column on mobile.

## Signature elements
- Circular bean-seal brand mark made from three abstract bean shapes.
- Small eyebrow labels in uppercase tracking.
- Thin rules, numbered menu rows, rounded image windows, and hand-drawn-style accent strokes via CSS.

## Interaction philosophy
Hover states should feel tactile: menu cards lift, images zoom slightly, and CTA arrows nudge. Navigation should remain simple and the phone link should always be one tap away.

## Animation
Reveal sections with subtle opacity + translateY, hero art with a slow scale settle, and a low-key floating accent. Disable non-essential motion under prefers-reduced-motion.

## Typography system
- Display: Cormorant Garamond, semibold, italic moments for warmth.
- UI/body: DM Sans, medium to regular.
- Labels: DM Sans, uppercase, 0.16em tracking.

## Brand essence
Trio Bean Cafe is an intimate pause: three beans, one table, a little more time.

## Brand voice
Warm, confident, lightly poetic, concise. Say “come by”, “slow mornings”, and “made to linger” rather than over-selling.

## Wordmark / logo
Use the name “TRIO BEAN” with a compact three-bean seal. The full project icon is a flat 1:1 PNG with a saffron cream full-bleed background and dark espresso bean silhouette.

## Signature brand color
Terracotta #B65F42.

## Planned visual assets
- Hero: editorial still life of a ceramic espresso cup, beans, linen, and a sunlit cafe table with generous text-safe space.
- Menu / gallery: art-directed supporting still lifes for a saffron latte, pastry plate, and cafe interior, each with a distinct placement.
- Project icon: three-bean seal, flat and legible at small sizes.
